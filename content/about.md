Portala Tools is a free collection of online utilities hosted at [portalaser.cn](https://portalaser.cn). We build small, focused tools for developers, writers and everyday users — JSON formatters, encoders, hash generators, converters, calculators and more — all designed to be opened, used and closed in seconds.

## Our Principle: Your Data Stays on Your Device

Every tool on this site is a pure front-end application. All processing happens locally inside your browser with JavaScript. We have no backend that accepts your data, no accounts, no tracking, and no server-side storage. Paste, convert, and close the tab — nothing was ever sent anywhere.

That is not a marketing line; it is the architecture of the site. You can verify it yourself: open any tool, disconnect from the internet, and it keeps working.

## What We Offer

- **50+ free tools**, organized into nine categories: Developer Tools, Text Tools, Encryption & Hash, Color Tools, Unit Converter, Data Format, Calculators, Time & Date and Generators.
- **No signup, no cost, no limits** — every tool is free forever.
- **Fast and lightweight** — a static site served through the Cloudflare global network, with no heavy scripts on the home page.
