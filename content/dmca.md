This page describes Portala Tools' policy for responding to claims of copyright infringement, in line with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512. Portala Tools is the operator of the website at https://portalaser.cn.

## What the DMCA Is

The DMCA is a United States copyright law that establishes a notice-and-takedown system for online service providers. It gives copyright holders a way to request removal of material that allegedly infringes their work, and it gives the parties who posted that material a way to file a counter-notification to have it restored. A valid notice must meet the statutory requirements below.

## Important Note: No User Uploads

Portala Tools is a set of purely client-side utilities. Our tools run entirely in your web browser; **we do not host user uploads, user accounts, comments, forums, or any user-submitted content.** The text you paste into a tool never reaches our servers. As a result, copyright claims on the Site are generally limited to our own original page content (such as written descriptions or design elements), not to content users generate in their own browser sessions.

## Filing a Takedown Notice

If you believe content on the Site infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated agent. To be effective under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your notice must include substantially all of the following:

1. **Identification of the copyrighted work** you claim has been infringed (include the title and, if relevant, multiple works covered by a single notice).
2. **Identification of the infringing material** and its exact URL(s) on https://portalaser.cn, sufficient for us to locate it.
3. **Your contact information**, including your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
4. **A good-faith statement** that you believe in good faith the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. **A statement under penalty of perjury** that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
6. **Your physical or electronic signature.**

## Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe your material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g). Your counter-notification should include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled, and its previous location before removal.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and an email address, plus a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the district where your address is located (or, if outside the United States, any judicial district in which the Site may be found).
5. A statement that you will accept service of process from the original complainant.

We will forward a valid counter-notification to the party who filed the original notice. If we do not receive notice within the statutory period that a lawsuit has been filed, we may restore the material.
