**Word Counter** is a free online tool that counts your text in real time. Paste or type into the box and you instantly see the number of words, total characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and an estimated reading time based on a comfortable 200 words per minute. It is built for writers, students, bloggers and marketers who need to stay under a length limit for essays, ads, meta descriptions or social posts.

There is nothing to install and nothing to submit. The counting happens entirely inside your browser the moment you type, so drafts and sensitive notes never leave your device - no upload, no tracking, no storage.

How to use it: just start typing or paste your draft on the left and watch the figures update live. Use **Example** to load a sample paragraph, or **Clear** to reset the box when you are done.
