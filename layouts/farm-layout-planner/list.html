Why a grid first?

A layout sketch is the fastest way to turn a vague farm idea into a visible constraint. It gives you a place to compare a crop block, a path, a building footprint, and a little breathing room before you spend in-game resources.

What it saves

The MVP saves the plan name and tile map in your browser. Exporting JSON gives you a small portable backup without requiring an account or sending a farm file to a server.
